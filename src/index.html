Differential Diagnosis of Adenocarcinoma vs Squamous Cell Carcinoma in Pulmonary Biopsies

World Health Organization Classification of Thoracic Tumours, 5th Edition (2021) | PMID: 33906806

§ 1.1 · IHC WORKUP

Diagnostic IHC Algorithm in Small Biopsies

In poorly differentiated non-small cell lung carcinomas lacking definitive morphological features of keratinization or glandular acinar formation, an initial tissue-sparing two-marker immunohistochemical panel consisting of TTF-1 and p40 is mandated.

TTF-1 (+) / p40 (−) Confirms pulmonary adenocarcinoma (~85% sensitivity, >97% specificity).
TTF-1 (−) / p40 (+) Confirms squamous cell carcinoma (~99% specificity across solid variants).
Discordant / Dual (−) Warrants reflex panel evaluation with Napsin A, CK5/6, and CK7 while preserving tissue for NGS.
§ 1.2 · ARCHITECTURAL GRADING

Histological Subtypes and Prognostic Grading

Invasive pulmonary adenocarcinoma is classified by predominant growth pattern: lepidic (Grade 1), acinar or papillary (Grade 2), and micropapillary or solid (Grade 3). Any tumor exhibiting ≥20% high-grade patterns (solid, micropapillary, or complex glandular cribriform) is classified as poorly differentiated (Grade 3) and correlates with elevated post-resection recurrence risk.

§ 2.1 · BIOMARKER SPECIFICITY

Superiority of p40 over p63 in Pulmonary Squamous Diagnosis

The ΔNp63 isoform-specific antibody p40 demonstrates equivalent sensitivity (~100%) to conventional polyclonal p63 for pulmonary squamous cell carcinoma while providing markedly superior specificity (~98% vs ~68%), avoiding false-positive squamous misclassifications in solid adenocarcinomas.

§ 3.1 · MOLECULAR STEWARDSHIP

Comprehensive Biomarker Interrogation & Tissue Stewardship

Routine multiplex molecular testing for EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF V600E, MET exon 14 skipping, RET, and KRAS G12C is indicated for all advanced-stage lung adenocarcinomas. Strict tissue stewardship during initial diagnostic IHC workup is mandatory to preserve FFPE block cellularity (≥20% neoplastic nuclei) for NGS interrogation.
