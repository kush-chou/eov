Differential Diagnosis of Adenocarcinoma vs Squamous Cell Carcinoma in Pulmonary Biopsies

World Health Organization (WHO) Classification of Thoracic Tumours, 5th Edition | PMID: 33906806

Diagnostic IHC Algorithm in Small Biopsies

In poorly differentiated non-small cell lung carcinomas lacking clear morphological features of keratinization or gland formation, an initial two-marker IHC panel consisting of TTF-1 (adenocarcinoma) and p40 (squamous cell carcinoma) is strongly recommended to conserve tissue.

  • TTF-1 (+) / p40 (-): Strongly supports pulmonary adenocarcinoma (~85% sensitivity).
  • TTF-1 (-) / p40 (+): Strongly supports squamous cell carcinoma (~99% specificity).
  • Dual positivity or negativity: Warrants extended reflex testing including Napsin A, CK5/6, and CK7.

Histological Patterns and Architectural Grading

Invasive pulmonary adenocarcinoma exhibits architectural heterogeneity with five primary growth patterns: lepidic, acinar, papillary, micropapillary, and solid. The presence of >=20% high-grade patterns (micropapillary or solid) correlates with aggressive clinical course and elevated risk of nodal metastasis.

Diagnosis of Lung Cancer in Small Biopsies and Cytology

Travis WD, et al. Journal of Thoracic Oncology 2011; 6(2):244-285 | PMID: 21317737

Superiority of p40 over p63 in Pulmonary Squamous Diagnosis

p40 (ΔNp63) offers equivalent sensitivity to p63 (~100%) for squamous cell carcinoma while providing dramatically higher specificity (~98% vs ~68%), eliminating false-positive diagnoses frequently observed with polyclonal p63 in adenocarcinomas and large-cell neuroendocrine carcinomas.

Updated Molecular Testing Guideline for Selection of Lung Cancer Patients for TKIs

Lindeman NI, et al. Arch Pathol Lab Med 2018; 142(3):321-346 | PMID: 29360357

Comprehensive Biomarker Interrogation & Tissue Stewardship

Routine multiplex molecular testing for EGFR mutations, ALK rearrangements, ROS1 fusions, and BRAF V600E is recommended for all advanced stage lung adenocarcinomas. Pathologists must enforce strict tissue stewardship during preliminary morphologic assessment to guarantee cellularity for NGS interrogation and PD-L1 immunohistochemistry.
