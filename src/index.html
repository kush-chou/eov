Differential Diagnosis of Adenocarcinoma vs Squamous Cell Carcinoma in Pulmonary Biopsies

World Health Organization (WHO) Classification of Thoracic Tumours, 5th Edition

Immunohistochemical Workup: In poorly differentiated non-small cell lung carcinomas lacking clear morphological features of keratinization or gland formation, an initial two-marker IHC panel consisting of TTF-1 (adenocarcinoma) and p40 (squamous cell carcinoma) is recommended.

Diagnostic Interpretation:

  • TTF-1 (+) / p40 (-): Strongly supports pulmonary adenocarcinoma.
  • TTF-1 (-) / p40 (+): Strongly supports squamous cell carcinoma.
  • Dual positivity or negativity warrants extended panel evaluation including Napsin A, CK5/6, and CK7.
